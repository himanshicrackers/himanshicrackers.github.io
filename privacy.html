Privacy Policy

How we handle the details you enter when preparing an order.

This website does not store your order. The name, phone number, address and notes you type are used only to build a WhatsApp message on your device. They are sent to us only if you press Send in WhatsApp. Your cart is kept in your own browser so it is still there if you return.

Messages you send us on WhatsApp are handled under WhatsApp's own terms. We use your details only to process and deliver your order.
