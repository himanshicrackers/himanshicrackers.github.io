Fireworks Safety Information

Use fireworks safely and legally.

  • Follow applicable local laws and official safety guidance.
  • Only adults should light fireworks. Supervise children closely.
  • Use outdoors in an open area, away from buildings, vehicles and dry grass.
  • Light one item at a time, at arm's length, and step back.
  • Never relight a dud. Soak it in water.
  • Keep a bucket of water nearby.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from flame.
  • Wear cotton clothes. Keep pets indoors.

Sale and use of fireworks may be restricted in your area. Check local rules before ordering.
